Party playlists are judged on floor response, not on taste. The governing attribute is
danceability rather than energy alone - a 0.95-energy metal track has enormous energy and
almost no danceability, because what people dance to is a strong, regular, predictable
downbeat with space between the hits.

The reliable tempo band is narrow: 115-130 BPM covers house, most pop, reggaeton, afrobeats
and disco, and it is narrow because it is a multiple of walking pace. Familiarity is also a
genuine feature here in a way it is not elsewhere - a crowd responds to recognition, so
popularity is a legitimate signal for a party and a misleading one for personal listening.

Variety matters more than coherence. One artist three times kills a room, and so does forty
minutes of the same genre, which makes the diversity penalty more valuable in this context
than in any other. A build arc works: start around 0.75 energy and climb.

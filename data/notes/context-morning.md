Morning listening has a gradient problem: the listener's arousal is low and rising, so the
right playlist is the one that leads them rather than meeting them. Starting at full party
energy at 7am is the classic failure - it gets skipped. Starting too low keeps them asleep.

The usable shape is a build: open around 0.55-0.65 energy and climb toward 0.8 over the
list, with tempo in the 100-120 BPM range, which is close to a brisk walking pace and
encourages movement without demanding it. Valence matters more here than in any other
context: brightness, major tonality and warmth in the vocal are doing the actual work, which
is why "sunny", "uplifting" and "hopeful" are better tags to match than "energetic".

Acousticness is neutral in the morning. What matters is that the first thirty seconds are
not abrasive.

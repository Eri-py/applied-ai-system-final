Concentration music works by not competing for the listening part of the brain. The
dominant finding in the attention literature is the irrelevant speech effect: intelligible
lyrics in a language you understand measurably degrade reading comprehension and verbal
working memory, while instrumental material of the same tempo and loudness does not. This is
the single strongest rule for a focus playlist - prefer instrumental, and if there are
vocals, prefer a language the listener does not speak or vocals treated as texture.

The second rule is dynamic flatness. A track with a dramatic drop or a sudden key change
pulls attention precisely because it is interesting. Lofi, ambient and minimal electronic
music are popular for study not because they are good but because they are predictable:
narrow dynamic range, looped structure, no surprises, tempo in the 70-90 BPM range which sits
below a resting heart rate and encourages stillness.

Energy around 0.35-0.45 is the usable band. Below that the music stops masking office noise,
which is one of its real jobs; above it the music becomes the activity.
